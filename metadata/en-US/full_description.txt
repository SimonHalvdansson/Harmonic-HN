Harmonic is a fully-featured, open-source, mature Hacker News client with a focus on (material) design, polish, and customization.

<b><u>Highlights</u></b>
• Material 3 Expressive design with smooth animations
• Full login functionality (vote, comment, submit, favorite, etc.)
• Strong theming support
• Quality of life features such as reader mode, adblock, link previews
• Excellent performance
• A large collection of settings for customizability

Harmonic has been developed over a long time and its main feature is the relentless polish and feature completeness it has accumulated.

Harmonic for Hacker News is <u>not</u> affiliated with Y Combinator.
